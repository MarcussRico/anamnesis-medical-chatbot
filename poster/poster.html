01Problem

Symptom checkers name the right condition first only about a third of the time [Semigran 2015]. Student projects on the standard Kaggle dataset claim 100% — but patients start a conversation with two symptoms, not a 132-field form.

02Leakage audit — our first finding

304unique rows in 4,920
100%of official test rows already in training
61.8%LR accuracy from 2 symptoms on unseen profiles

The famous 100% is memorisation. We split by unique profile, so no test patient has a copy in training.

03Objectives

  • Repair the evaluation protocol (grouped split)
  • Measure accuracy under partial evidence
  • Ask each follow-up question by expected information gain
  • Lay-language NLU with negation; warning-sign triage
  • No generated text — nothing can be hallucinated

05Method

p(d | E) ∝ p(d) · Πpresent qds · Πabsent (1 − qds) — unknown symptoms drop out

Each turn scores all 132 unasked symptoms by expected entropy reduction (0.18 ms) and asks the best one; stops at 90% confidence, abstains below 50%.
